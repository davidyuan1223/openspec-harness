Load and follow the skill `openspec-harness-explore` for: $ARGUMENTS

Do not create implementation files. Identify business scenarios, assumptions,
open questions, acceptance risks, and likely validation signals. Ensure
`openspec/harness/test-context.md` exists or draft it from the project testing
environment. For frontend-visible work, note that later validation must exercise
the running application.
